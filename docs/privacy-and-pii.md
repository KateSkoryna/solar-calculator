# Privacy and PII inventory

Every place personal data or secrets are stored, who can read them, how long they live and whether they may reach logs. Logs go through `lib/logger.ts`, which redacts keys listed in `PII_KEYS` (`lib/redact-pii.ts`) and replaces email addresses in strings with `[EMAIL]`.

| Data item                                                                        | Where stored                                                                         | Classification                                                | Who can read it                                            | Retention                                                                | Can appear in logs                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| `User.id`                                                                        | `User.id`                                                                            | Non-personal identifier                                       | The user, fleet members through relations, database admins | Until the account is deleted                                             | Yes                               |
| `User.email`                                                                     | `User.email`                                                                         | PII                                                           | The user, database admins                                  | Until the account is deleted                                             | No, redacted                      |
| `User.password`                                                                  | `User.password` (bcrypt hash)                                                        | Secret                                                        | Nobody in the app; database admins see only the hash       | Until the account is deleted or the password is reset                    | No, redacted                      |
| `User.name`                                                                      | `User.name`                                                                          | PII                                                           | The user, fleet members                                    | Until the account is deleted                                             | No                                |
| `User.emailVerified`                                                             | `User.emailVerified`                                                                 | Non-personal timestamp                                        | The user, database admins                                  | Until the account is deleted                                             | Yes                               |
| `User.image`                                                                     | `User.image` (URL from the sign-in provider)                                         | PII                                                           | The user, database admins                                  | Until the account is deleted                                             | No                                |
| `User.resetToken`                                                                | `User.resetToken`                                                                    | Secret                                                        | Nobody in the app; database admins                         | One hour (`resetTokenExpiry`), cleared after use                         | No, redacted                      |
| `User.resetTokenExpiry`                                                          | `User.resetTokenExpiry`                                                              | Non-personal timestamp                                        | Database admins                                            | Cleared after use                                                        | Yes                               |
| `User.createdAt`, `User.updatedAt`                                               | `User`                                                                               | Non-personal timestamps                                       | The user, database admins                                  | Until the account is deleted                                             | Yes                               |
| `Account.access_token`                                                           | `Account.access_token`                                                               | Secret                                                        | Nobody in the app; database admins                         | Until the account is unlinked or deleted                                 | No, redacted                      |
| `Account.refresh_token`                                                          | `Account.refresh_token`                                                              | Secret                                                        | Nobody in the app; database admins                         | Until the account is unlinked or deleted                                 | No, redacted                      |
| `Account.id_token`                                                               | `Account.id_token`                                                                   | Secret                                                        | Nobody in the app; database admins                         | Until the account is unlinked or deleted                                 | No, redacted                      |
| `Account.providerAccountId`                                                      | `Account.providerAccountId`                                                          | PII (provider user id)                                        | Database admins                                            | Until the account is unlinked or deleted                                 | No                                |
| `Account.provider`, `type`, `token_type`, `scope`, `expires_at`, `session_state` | `Account`                                                                            | Non-personal                                                  | Database admins                                            | Until the account is unlinked or deleted                                 | Yes                               |
| `VerificationToken.identifier`                                                   | `VerificationToken.identifier` (the email address)                                   | PII                                                           | Database admins                                            | Until `expires`, deleted on use                                          | No                                |
| `VerificationToken.token`                                                        | `VerificationToken.token`                                                            | Secret                                                        | Database admins                                            | Until `expires`, deleted on use                                          | No, redacted                      |
| `VerificationToken.expires`                                                      | `VerificationToken.expires`                                                          | Non-personal timestamp                                        | Database admins                                            | Until deleted on use                                                     | Yes                               |
| `FleetMembership.userId`, `fleetId`                                              | `FleetMembership`                                                                    | Non-personal identifiers that link a person to a fleet        | Fleet members, database admins                             | Removed when the user is deleted (cascade)                               | Yes                               |
| `FleetMembership.role`, `createdAt`                                              | `FleetMembership`                                                                    | Non-personal                                                  | Fleet members                                              | Removed when the user is deleted (cascade)                               | Yes                               |
| `AuditEvent.metadata`                                                            | `AuditEvent.metadata` (JSON: reason, allowed roles, actual role)                     | Non-personal by rule; must never hold emails, names or tokens | Fleet owners, database admins                              | Kept for the life of the fleet; append-only                              | Yes, still redacted by the logger |
| `AuditEvent.actorUserId`                                                         | `AuditEvent.actorUserId`                                                             | Non-personal identifier                                       | Fleet owners                                               | Kept for the life of the fleet                                           | Yes                               |
| `Vehicle.city`                                                                   | `Vehicle.city`                                                                       | Business data that can reveal a depot location                | Fleet members                                              | Until the vehicle is deleted (soft delete via `deletedAt`)               | Avoid                             |
| `Vehicle.country`                                                                | `Vehicle.country`                                                                    | Non-personal                                                  | Fleet members                                              | Until the vehicle is deleted                                             | Yes                               |
| Session cookie                                                                   | `authjs.session-token` (`__Secure-` prefix on Vercel), `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`    | Secret                                                        | The browser and the server only                            | Session lifetime set in `auth.ts`                                        | No, the `cookie` key is redacted  |
| Rate-limiter IP address                                                          | In-memory map key in `lib/rate-limit.ts`, taken from `x-forwarded-for` in `proxy.ts` | PII (IP address)                                              | The running server process only                            | About one rate-limit window, then dropped; never written to the database | No                                |
